Support

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Do you have a question, found a bug, or have a suggestion about one of our apps? We're happy to help.

1 Contact

Email us at LNSB-Publishing@outlook.com. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

To help us assist you quickly, please include:
  • The name of the app
  • Operating system and version (e.g. iOS 18.2, Android 15)
  • A short description of the problem, ideally with a screenshot

2 Frequently Asked Questions

The app crashes or is unresponsive

Restart the app and check whether an update is available in the App Store or Google Play Store. If the problem persists, write to us with a brief description of when exactly the crash occurs.

How do I disable personalized advertising?

On iOS via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android via Settings → Google → Ads. Within the EU/EEA/UK/CH you can also adjust your consent directly in the app's consent window. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I have my data deleted?

Email us with the name of the app – we will delete the data we hold about your request. Advertising-related data held by Google is subject to Google's own deletion periods.

I have an idea for a new feature

We welcome feedback and suggestions – just send them to us by email.
