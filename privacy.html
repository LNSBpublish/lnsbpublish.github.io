Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by LNSB-Publishing on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, unless stated otherwise within the respective app.

1 Data Controller

The party responsible for data processing within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

LNSB-Publishing
Email: LNSB-Publishing@outlook.com

2 What data our apps process

Our apps generally only process the data necessary for their operation and for displaying advertising. If a particular app includes additional features (e.g. a user account, cloud sync, or its own servers), you will find supplementary information directly within that app.

As a rule, the following data is processed:

  • Technical device data (e.g. device model, operating system and version, language setting)
  • Usage data within the app (e.g. crash reports, general interaction data)
  • The device's advertising ID (see Section 3)

3 Advertising via Google AdMob

Our apps integrate the advertising service Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). AdMob displays advertisements within the app and thereby finances the free or discounted use of the app.

Legal basis

Within the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we obtain your consent via a consent management tool (Google User Messaging Platform, UMP) before personalized advertising is served. In this case, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time via the relevant settings in the app or on your device. Outside these regions, processing may be based on our legitimate interest in an ad-financed app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Recipients and transfers to third countries

The recipient of the data is Google (Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC, USA). For transfers to the USA, Google states that it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as an appropriate safeguard within the meaning of Art. 46 GDPR. Further information can be found in Google's Privacy Policy for partner sites and apps and in AdMob's privacy information.

Disabling or limiting advertising

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt on first launch.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of interest-based ads".
  • Within the EU/EEA/UK/CH you can also adjust your consent directly via the consent window in the app itself.

4 Children and Minors

Our apps are not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age, unless explicitly stated otherwise within the respective app. Should one of our apps be intended for a younger audience, advertising delivery is configured accordingly as "directed at children" and no personalized ads based on the advertising identifier are served.

5 Retention Period

We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, or as long as required by statutory retention obligations. Advertising-related data is processed by Google according to its own deletion periods.

6 Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your data violates the GDPR.

To exercise your rights, an informal email to LNSB-Publishing@outlook.com is sufficient. Please indicate the name of the app concerned.

7 Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, complete security in the transmission of data over the internet cannot be guaranteed.

8 Changes to this Privacy Policy

We update this privacy policy whenever our apps, the services we use, or legal requirements change. The current version can always be found at this address.

9 Contact

We are happy to answer questions about this privacy policy by email: LNSB-Publishing@outlook.com
