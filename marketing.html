Pulse Stack

Stack higher. Stay in rhythm.

A minimalist 3D stacking game. One tap, perfect timing, an endless tower – how high can you go?

Download on the App Store See features

Simple to learn. Hard to master.

One-tap gameplay

Tap to drop the moving block. Line it up well and your tower keeps its size; miss and it gets trimmed.

Real 3D look

A clean 3D view with smooth animation and a colour palette that shifts as your tower grows.

30 block designs

Collect coins as you play and unlock new looks for your blocks. Some designs unlock by watching optional rewarded ads.

Leaderboards & achievements

Compete with friends and players worldwide through Apple Game Center.

Second chance

Use revive credits to continue a great run instead of starting over.

Your progress is saved

Best score, coins and unlocked designs are stored on your device. No account needed.

Three taps to get hooked

  1. 1

    Watch the block

    A block slides back and forth above your tower.

  2. 2

    Tap at the right time

    Stop it exactly on top. Perfect timing keeps your tower wide.

  3. 3

    Climb higher

Questions & answers

Is Pulse Stack free?

Yes. You can download and play for free. Optional in-app purchases let you remove ads or buy revive credits.

Do I need an account?

No. Pulse Stack does not ask you to create an account. Apple Game Center is used for leaderboards and achievements.
